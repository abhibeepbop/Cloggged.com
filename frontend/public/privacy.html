This Privacy Policy explains how Cloggged ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our email classification service (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Process

  • Gmail data (via Google APIs): We access your Gmail with your consent to classify messages, apply labels, and move obvious spam to Gmail’s Spam folder.
  • Email content: Processed transiently in memory for classification. We do not store email bodies or attachments.
  • Metadata we may store: Gmail message IDs, label IDs/names, classification result (e.g., category, confidence), and timestamps required to operate, deduplicate, and provide usage stats.
  • Account & billing: Email address, subscription status, Stripe identifiers, and basic billing timestamps.
  • Technical: Basic logs (e.g., errors, performance, job runs) to maintain and secure the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Classify and label your Gmail messages and move obvious spam to Gmail’s Spam folder.
